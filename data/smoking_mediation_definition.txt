Current mediation example: later_tob is later_tobacco_exposure, average cigarettes/day across months 1–12. It includes zero for smoke-free months. Exposure is seven-day cessation at baseline; cancer is assessed after year 1 through year 20. The original cohort is unchanged. The simple linear mediator and logistic outcome models illustrate the two-model workflow. The zero-heavy mediator is poorly represented by a Gaussian model: this is a teaching approximation requiring explicit limitations, not a validated causal analysis. A two-part or other suitable mediator distribution is needed for substantive analysis. A previously considered month-12 abstinence example is not the current primary mediation example.
